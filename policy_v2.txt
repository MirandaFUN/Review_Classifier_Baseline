2. Content Policy

2.1 Definition

Our policy prohibits fake, manipulated, and misleading incentivized product reviews that misrepresent a reviewer's actual experience or unfairly influence customers' perceptions of a product.

A review violates our policy if it falls into any of the following categories:

1. Fabricated experience:

Claiming to have used a product when available evidence contradicts that claim, or providing only generic and vague praise or criticism，or mention personal needs, purchases, or conversations or unrelated details without providing meaningful.

We define what is meaningful as:

reviews should provide relevant information that helps other customers make informed purchasing decisions from error 3) information about actual product use or purchase experience.

Only describe product features without any first-person experience. Look for contextual details about how the reviewer used the product, rather than relying solely on the specificity of product descriptions

Meaningful feedback may include a specific usage context, how the product was used, sensory observations, product attributes, or outcomes. Examples include comfort, texture, fit, ease of cleaning, installation, durability, or performance. A review does not need to include all of these details, and brief feedback can be meaningful.

The following are types of reviews that we don't allow and will remove:

- A review containing claims about usage duration that do not match the verified purchase date, such as stating "used for 3 months" when the product was purchased two days ago.
- AI-generated reviews unsupported by genuine experience and exaggerated results.
- A review consisting entirely of generic praise or criticism, with no meaningful information about the product or purchase experience. However, a brief comment about a specific attribute, such as “soft” or “easy to clean,” is permitted.

2. Undisclosed incentives:

Failing to disclose rewards such as money, discounts, or free products received for posting a review, or offering incentives in exchange for a specific rating or positive reviews of products never used.

Reviews provided in exchange for incentives conditional on a specific rating constitute a violation, even if they acknowledge the product’s shortcomings. Ordinary purchase discounts are not review incentives unless tied to posting a review. Reviews that disclose or criticize sellers offering rewards for specific ratings should not be classified as violations solely for mentioning such incentives(from error analysis 7)

3. Manipulated reviews:

Artificially increasing or decreasing product ratings through coordinated reviews/review farms.

4. Misleading AI-Generated Media:

Using AI-generated or modified images and videos to misrepresent a product's appearance or performance, such as exaggerating before-and-after photos or concealing defects. The use of AI alone does not constitute a violation.

5. Disguised Promotional Content:

Disguising commercial promotion as genuine user recommendations, including posing as customers to criticize products or promote competitors, directing or recommending users to other stores, platforms, or accounts(especially those that are in a competitive relationship) .And sharing undisclosed promotional discount codes and purchase links.

Presenting commercial promotion as an independent recommendation while concealing a commercial relationship or personal benefit. Product comparisons used to promote an alternative are also violations.

Permitted Reviews:

Genuine reviews, including those edited with AI, are allowed. Relevant feedback may cover product attributes, price, purchase context, packaging, delivery, or comparisons. Honest first impressions are allowed, as are disclosed incentives without an evidenced rating requirement.
